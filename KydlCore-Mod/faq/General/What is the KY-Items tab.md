What is the KY-Items tab?

A list of every item and piece the installed KydlDev mods add, as a list or as a grid of big icons (switch at the top right; the choice is remembered in [General] KY-Items View). Deposits and crafting ingredients are on a separate Materials tab. The tabs only show when a KydlDev mod adds such things. All KydlDev build pieces are in one hammer category, KY-Items.
