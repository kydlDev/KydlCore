What does KydlCore do?

KydlCore is the shared base of every KydlDev mod. It has no gameplay of its own: it gives all KydlDev mods one menu (the KY hub, F6), one look for their windows, the KY-Items build category and list, and a flight recorder that writes a short report when something goes wrong. Your mod manager installs it together with the KydlDev mods that need it.
