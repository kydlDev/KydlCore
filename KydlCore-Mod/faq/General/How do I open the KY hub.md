How do I open the KY hub?

Press F6 in game. The first row lists the installed KydlDev mods, the second row their tabs (settings and the mod's own pages). Escape closes the window, and your character does not move while it is open. The key can be changed with [General] HubKey (in the hub's own Settings tab or in BepInEx/config/com.kydldev.KydlCore.cfg); None means no key.
