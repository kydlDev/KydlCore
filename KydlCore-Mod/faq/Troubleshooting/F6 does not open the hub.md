F6 does not open the hub, what do I check?

The hub does not open while the chat, the console or the game menu is open: close them and press the key again. Check [General] HubKey in BepInEx/config/com.kydldev.KydlCore.cfg: it may have been changed to another key or to None (no key). If it still does not open, see "How do I report a bug?".
