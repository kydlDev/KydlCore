How do I report a bug?

Turn on [DebugLog] Enable (hub Settings tab or the .cfg file), make the problem happen again, and attach BepInEx/LogOutput.log. If BepInEx/cache/KydlCore/flight/ holds a report file from that time, attach it too. The quickest place to report is the KydlDev Discord: https://discord.gg/tX2CcTNC4W
